1. Overview

RemoteKey ("the Application") is designed to allow users to control their personal computer (PC) remotely using their Android smartphone via local Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth connections. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No External Servers: The Application does not use any cloud servers, telemetry, or external tracking services.
  • Local Network Only: All data transmissions (mouse/keyboard input commands, screen control signals, and PC audio streaming) occur strictly within your own private Local Area Network (LAN) or direct Bluetooth connection between your phone and your PC.

3. Permissions Used by the Application

The Application requests specific Android device permissions solely to provide core functionality:

  • Network / Wi-Fi (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Used exclusively to discover and connect to your PC on your local network via TCP/UDP sockets.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE): Used to function as a driverless Bluetooth HID keyboard/mouse controller.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used strictly for scanning the QR code displayed on your PC screen for instant pairing. Camera data is processed locally on-device and never recorded or transmitted.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used to display a persistent status notification while streaming PC audio in the background or during active sessions, preventing the OS from disconnecting your session when the screen turns off.
  • Sensors (HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS): Used to provide Air Mouse / Gyroscope mouse movement control.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application does not share any user data with third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or external service providers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at:
